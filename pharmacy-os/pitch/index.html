01 The problem

India’s medicines reach people through small medical stores. They run on software from another era.

Nine of every ten medical stores are run by one owner. He orders, receives, bills, files GST and keeps the law, mostly by hand or in old desktop software. Every gap costs him money, and it can cost him his licence.
