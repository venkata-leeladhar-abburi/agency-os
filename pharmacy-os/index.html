01The gap

One medicine. Typed three times.

We talked with store owners in October 2026. Their software works. The typing never ends.

Typed 3 times

    Today’s steps come from our first store visits and the products’ own guides. Yours may differ.

    The trade asked for this in 2021

    Send a file, the chemists wrote, “to avoid manual punching.”

    The All India Organisation of Chemists & Druggists, in a letter to drug makers on 20 January 2021. It also asked that the batch number on the bill match the stock in the carton. Five years on, shops still type.
