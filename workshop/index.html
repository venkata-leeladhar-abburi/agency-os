Seven tells

The one-page spec
Seven fixes, decided before the prompt
  • Purple gradientOne flat ground
  • Emoji iconsOne icon set
  • Everything centredLeft-aligned grid
  • “Welcome to my website”Says what you do
  • Three identical cardsVaried layout
  • Default fontTwo chosen fonts
  • Glow on every boxFlat surfaces
  1. 01A purple-to-blue gradient on everythingOne flat ground and one accent colour
  2. 02Emoji used as iconsOne icon set such as Lucide, or none
  3. 03Everything centredLeft-aligned words on a grid
  4. 04“Welcome to my website”Say what you do and for whom, first line
  5. 05Three identical cards in every sectionA list, a split, a full-width band
  6. 06A default font in one weightTwo fonts and clear jumps in size
  7. 07Glow and shadow on every boxFlat surfaces, a hairline for an edge

Scroll-animation site: the page is a film

Students watch one scroll story come together and take the prompt home. Nobody builds, so nobody falls behind.

Build 220 minGSAP · Rive · LottieFiles · Spline60fps.design · Awwwards

You teach

  • Five moves make up almost every award-winning scroll site. Learn the names and you can ask for them.
  • One idea per screen, and three moves a page at most.
  • Motion follows the finger, not a timer. Slow and short reads as premium.
  • Test on a phone, and switch motion off for people who ask for less.

Students do

  • Play “name the move” in chat while you scroll a real site.
  • Save the prompt pack.

The five moves

Learn the names and you can ask for them

  • PinA section stays fixed while the page keeps scrolling behind it.
  • ScrubThe animation’s progress is tied to the scroll position.
  • RevealWords and images fade or slide in as they enter the screen.
  • StaggerItems arrive one after another, a tenth of a second apart.
  • ParallaxLayers move at different speeds, which reads as depth.

One scroll story on four screens

This strip is scrubbed. Scroll!

  1. RevealThe name fills the screen, then fades as you scroll.
  2. Pin and scrubThe bag stays put while three facts change.
  3. StaggerSix photos arrive one after another.
  4. ParallaxThe background drifts slower than the words.

A dashboard follows different rules from a landing page and needs different references. That contrast is the lesson.

Build 315 minUntitled UI · Magic UI · 21st.devMobbin · Refero

You teach
