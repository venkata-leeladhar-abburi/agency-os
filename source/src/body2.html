        What to build, in what order

        We run our own agency on it first, then fill the gaps we found, then build the parts nobody else has.

        1. NOWUse it ourselves
          • Run One Stop Solutions projects on Agency OS
          • Fill our Playbook: process, prompts, tools
          • Log every friction point

          Why first: we sell what we use, and our own profile becomes the first showcase.

        2. R1Grow
          • Leads pipeline & sources
          • Lead → client in one click
          • Quotations & proposals
          • Intake forms
          • Google Calendar & Meet

          Why: the gap we found. Without it, onboarding starts with retyping.

        3. R2Playbook
          • All nine item types
          • Private, Team, Client, Public
          • Phase templates → projects
          • Figma & Miro boards

          Why: our edge, and the content that makes profiles worth visiting.

        4. R3Get found
          • Public profiles & How-we-work pages
          • Verified reviews & badge
          • Free tools
          • Hirer sign-up & marketplace
          • Solo plan

          Why now: profiles need Playbook content and portal reviews before hirers arrive.

        5. R4Deliver+
          • Document studio: PRD, SOW, NDA, MSA
          • Approval gates & change requests
          • Time tracking & payouts
          • Expenses, retainers & GST

          Why: deepens daily use once teams are inside.

        6. R5Premium & handoff
          • Handoff package
          • International client mode
          • Weekly status reports
          • Portal on own domain

          Why: what justifies agency pricing.

        7. R6Scale
          • AI first drafts
          • Android app, installable web app first
          • Meta, Google Ads & IndiaMART sync
          • Requirement briefs from hirers

          Why last: growth features once the core loop works.

        8. R7Mobile appsFuture scope
          • Native Android & iOS apps
          • Push notifications
          • Offline drafts that sync
          • Camera capture for receipts & assets
          • Home-screen widgets

          Why: leads, approvals, payments and check-ins happen away from a desk.

          See the mobile scope
        Future scopeAfter R6

        In your pocket

        Not part of the current build. R6 starts with an installable web app; native apps follow, designed around the moments that happen away from a desk: a new lead, an approval, a payment, a check-in.

        Concept screens

        Agency owner & project lead

        Must see on the phone

        • New-lead alerts with one-tap reply and a call-booking link
        • Today: meetings, follow-ups and dues
        • Revenue, pending and overdue at a glance
        • Approve quotes, change requests and leave
        • Projects at risk and team load
        • Request a client approval at a phase gate

        Team member & HR

        Must see on the phone

        • Attendance check-in
        • Today’s tasks and a time-log timer
        • Daily update form in under a minute
        • Team chat and files
        • Leave, payslips and notices
        • Leave approvals for HR

        Client

        Must see on the phone

        • Progress, dues and what needs them
        • Approve or comment at each phase gate
        • Pay invoices inside the app
        • View and sign contracts
        • Chat with the agency
        • Handoff page and acceptance sign-off

        Hirer

        Must see on the phone

        • Browse by service, city, budget and rating
        • Saved agencies and shortlists
        • Enquiries and replies in one inbox
        • Request a quote in two steps
        • Same login becomes their client portal
        Built into every app
        • Push notifications, with email & WhatsApp fallbacks
        • Offline drafts that sync
        • Two-factor & biometric login
        • Camera capture for receipts, brand assets & documents
        • Home-screen widgets for today & the timer
        • Deep links from every notification
        • In-app payments via Razorpay
