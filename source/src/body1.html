          ( The strategy )

          Give freelancers a reason to join before they ever pay

          Free profiles, free tools and a free starter workspace bring freelancers and agencies in. Hirers browse those profiles and send work. Every finished project makes the profile stronger, and growth moves them onto a paid plan.

          • Free, forever

            • Public profile and marketplace listing
            • Verified reviews and a badge for their own site
            • Free tools: invoices, calculators, PRD and SOW generators
            • Starter Playbook, CRM and one client portal

          • Never charged

            • No commission on any project
            • Hirers never pay to browse or contact
            • Listings never expire

          • Why they upgrade

            • More clients than the free limit
            • A team to manage, delegate to and pay
            • Branded documents and portals for every client
            • Handoff packages and foreign clients
